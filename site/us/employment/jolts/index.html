How many jobs are open, how many people move into them, and how many leave — the flows behind the payroll count.

Table 3Vacancies per unemployed personRatio, s.a.

Openings divided by the number of unemployed. Above one, there are more vacancies than jobseekers — the tightness measure the Fed watches.

Table 4Beveridge curveRates, s.a.

The vacancy rate against the unemployment rate, traced in time order. Movement along the curve is the cycle; a shift of the whole curve is a change in how well the labour market matches people to jobs.

Table 5Hires against total separationsPercent of employment, s.a.

Employment grows only while hires exceed separations. The gap between these two lines is the payroll change.
